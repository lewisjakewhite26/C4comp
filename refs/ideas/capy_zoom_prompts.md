# Pond-to-mug macro zoom: Veo prompts (27 Oct 2026)

Six 8 s shots that chain into one continuous dive for a vertical Short. Generate each one in Veo from the
**last frame of the shot before**, so the joins line up. Settings for all six: 9:16, 8 s.

End every prompt with this style line so the shots match:

> Soft storybook illustration, gentle watercolour lighting, cosy lofi mood, smooth continuous camera motion, no cuts.

## 1. The pond (text-to-video)

> A misty pond at dusk in light rain. A relaxed capybara floats peacefully on the water, eyes half closed, beside a large round green lily pad. Soft lantern light glows on the far bank and raindrops make gentle ripples. The camera slowly glides forward, low over the water, towards the lily pad. On its edge sits a single large, sparkling dewdrop catching the light.

## 2. Into the dewdrop (start from shot 1's last frame)

> The camera pushes in steadily and speeds up towards the single sparkling dewdrop on the edge of the lily pad. The capybara and pond blur softly behind. The droplet grows until it fills the whole frame, showing a curved, upside-down reflection of the misty pond and lantern lights inside it.

## 3. Through the droplet (start from shot 2's last frame)

> The camera passes straight through the surface of the water droplet. Inside, everything is a dreamy world of refracted light: swirling soft caustics, tiny floating bubbles and rainbow glints. The cool blue-green tones slowly warm into amber and cream as a smooth glossy curved surface starts to appear through the shimmer.

## 4. The mug (start from shot 3's last frame)

> The shimmer clears into an extreme close-up of the glossy side of a cream ceramic mug with a teal and pink zigzag band. Warm lamplight reflects on the glaze and wisps of steam drift past at the top of the frame. At the rim, a single dark drop of coffee gathers and begins to slide down.

## 5. The drip (start from shot 4's last frame)

> The camera follows a single dark drop of coffee as it runs slowly down the glossy cream mug, over the teal and pink zigzag pattern, leaving a thin shiny trail. The camera tracks downwards with the drop at the same steady pace. The glaze texture is crisp and the steam is soft.

## 6. The reveal (start from shot 5's last frame)

> The coffee drop reaches the base of the mug and the camera slowly pulls back and away. It reveals the mug steaming on a wooden side table beside a cosy sofa, where a happy capybara sits wrapped in a chunky pink and teal knitted blanket. Behind it, rain runs down a big window looking out on a glowing city at night. A warm table lamp glows.

Shot 6 ends on the blanket room from the 2-hour rainy night video, so the Short leads into it. For an exact match,
also give Veo the thumbnail frame (loop at 1.0 s) as shot 6's end frame.
