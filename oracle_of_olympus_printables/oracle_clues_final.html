Clue III · Table Tennis Table Blood is Thicker Than Water

The Oracle knows that family members stand first in line to inherit. But family members also know the most secrets. Alexios had blood relatives in Athens on the night he died — and one of them may not have told the full truth.

Look at your Citizens Database. Go through the Relationship column carefully. Decide which relationships count as a blood relative — not all of them do. Cross-check with the In Athens that night? column.

How many blood relatives were present in Athens on the night of the death?

Write all their names on your data sheet before you answer.
HINT Think carefully — not every family relationship means a blood relation. And make sure your database is complete. Did you fill in both missing witnesses from your scrolls?

✦ Note this down on your data sheet
The five blood relatives in Athens were Nikias, Lydia, Phoebe, Agathon and Sostrate. Note: Euandros (stepbrother) and Philon (brother-in-law) are NOT blood relatives.

Clue V · Storytelling Chair The Debtors of Alexios

Alexios was generous with his money — perhaps too generous. Several citizens owed him drachma at the time of his death. But not all of them were even in Athens. The Oracle wants to know who was away.

Look at your Citizens Database. Find all citizens who were NOT in Athens on the night of the death but who still have a debt recorded against them.

How many citizens match both conditions — not in Athens AND has a debt?

Make sure your database is complete before you answer. Check your second scroll.
HINT Your second witness from the scrolls was not in Athens that night — but he did owe Alexios money. If he is missing from your database, add him now before you answer.

✦ Well done — now unlock the final secret
The three citizens not in Athens with a debt were Sostrates, Eirene, and Bardas. If Bardas was missing, add him now from your scroll.

Now solve one last puzzle to complete your investigation.
Final Puzzle · Storytelling Chair The Secret in the Scrolls

Alexios was known to hide messages in plain sight. The Oracle has found a stack of books — but they are not here. They have been seen elsewhere in the school.

Make your way to the display board outside the Class 2 toilets.

Look carefully at the books on the poster. The first letter of each book title, read from top to bottom, spells a secret word.

Come back here and type the word to complete your investigation.
