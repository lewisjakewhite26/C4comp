# Colour sorting timer, 5 minutes, widescreen (YouTube)

520 soft glowing beads in rainbow colours start scattered. Matching colours drift together into six clumps, then the clumps spread and join into one rainbow ring that closes at 0:00. 1920x1080, 30 fps, 5:12 (3 s title card, 5:00 countdown, about 9 s hold with the ring slowly turning).
Sound: soft pad, quiet chimes pitched by the colour of each clump as it grows, a chime each minute, warmer chimes every 10 s in the last minute, a rainbow shimmer and singing bowl at 0:00.
