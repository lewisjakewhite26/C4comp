# Wind beads: 1 minute countdown

Multicoloured beads are blown across the screen by sweeping gusts and settle into a pile that rises with the countdown, filling the whole screen at 00:00. 1080p, 30 fps, 66 seconds (60 second countdown plus a gentle "Time's up" ending). Soft ambient sound with wind and chimes tied to the landing beads, and a chime at zero.

- `sim.py`: physics simulation (wind field, falling beads, sand pile)
- `render.py`: draws the frames and the timer
- `audio.py`: builds the soundtrack
