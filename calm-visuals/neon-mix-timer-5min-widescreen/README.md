# Neon liquid mix timer, 5 minutes, widescreen (YouTube)

Four neon blobs (blue, green, pink, purple) swap tiny beads of colour and slowly blend into one speckled liquid ball as the countdown runs. 1920x1080, 30 fps, 5:12 (3 s title card, 5:00 countdown, about 8 s hold at 0:00).
Sound: soft pad, very quiet chimes, a chime each minute, warmer chimes every 10 s in the last minute, singing bowl at 0:00.
